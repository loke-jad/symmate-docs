# Symmate · Manifesto Video Script

**Working title:** *Knowing, in Aggregate*
**Format:** Short manifesto / vision film
**Target runtime:** ~85 seconds (full cut). 30-sec cutdown at bottom.
**Angle:** Emotional, big-picture. The bartender metaphor is the spine.
**Source material:** `symmate-3.md`, `TheRegistry.txt`, `Sygil Collective Financial.txt`, `Symmate-2.md`.

---

## Tone & voice

Declarative, morally clear, quietly defiant — *not* hype. The docs already have the voice:
"The rails are neutral. The operator's intent is not." / "It reports to the member, not on the member." /
"Same plumbing, different building." Lift that register. Confident, plain, unhurried. No exclamation marks.

**Music:** Single sustained pad/drone that *builds* — sparse and lonely at the open (the exclusion), warmth
entering at the Symmate beat, a collective swell at the Group Mind, resolved and steady on the close.
No drop, no trailer-boom. Let silence land the key lines.

**Pace:** Slow holds early (let the problem breathe), quicker cuts through the architecture, hold again on the close.
VO ≈ 200 words ≈ comfortable at 85s with breathing room.

## Production notes

- **VO casting:** warm, grounded, unhurried — closer to a documentary narrator than an ad read. Could be the
  Mox voice if you want it in-universe. The HyperFrames `tts` command can rough-cut a scratch track for timing.
- **Captions:** the on-screen text above doubles as caption beats — keep them as *statements*, not subtitles of the VO.
- **Logo:** `symmates3.png` is the approved 1.2 MB / 1008×1008 asset in this folder. Reserve it for the end card; don't dilute it earlier.
- **Hard rule from the brand:** no surveillance imagery for the Symmate. It is a shield and an advocate, not an eye.
  Whenever a frame risks reading as "monitoring," flip it to "guarding."
